THE MODEL, MADE EXPLICIT
Equations with boundaries.
A finite computational interpretation of a fictional multiverse, developed under EFMW Physics. The first four experiments use standard quantum mathematics. The last two explore a normalized source equation and an explicitly proposed feedback rule.

        
01 / STATE
World × record
The state space is ℂᴺ ⊗ ℂ², with N = 2, 4, or 6. A density matrix represents probabilities and correlations. A one-bit register stands in for a minimal carried record; it is not a model of human consciousness.
Hₐₘ,ᵦₙ = δₘₙ[δₐᵦ(εₐ + ηm vₐ + gφₐ) + κAₐᵦBₐᵦ]
02 / EVOLUTION
Unitary transport + dephasing
Real symmetric Hamiltonians are diagonalized numerically. Each frozen step uses U = exp(−iHΔt), with dephasing half-steps on either side. No probability renormalization hides numerical drift.
ρ → DΔt/2(U DΔt/2(ρ) U†)
03 / FIELD
A periodic scalar domain
The classical field uses 128 points across a length-24 domain, velocity Verlet, and split damping. The Courant ratio is checked. The periodic zero mode may accumulate under sustained nonzero-mean forcing.
φ̈ + 2ζφ̇ − c²φ″ = −S
04 / INTERPRETATION
A simulator, with testable limits
Portal links and field feedback are modeling assumptions. This project does not derive television episodes, establish travel between universes, or validate EFMW as a law of nature.
Numerical agreement ≠ empirical confirmation
